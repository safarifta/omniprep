Frequently asked questions
Can I use OmniPrep on my phone? ▼

No. OmniPrep Works on desktop or laptop for the CBT Simulator since JAMB exams are taken on desktop computers.

How do I pay? I don't have a card. ▼

Paystack supports bank transfer payment methods. You do not need a card to pay.

When does my access expire? ▼

Your access lasts one full year from the date you pay. Since JAMB is an annual exam, this covers your entire preparation cycle. You can OmniPrep is free — no renewal payment needed.

Are the questions the same as the real JAMB? ▼

Our question bank is built to match the difficulty, style, and subject coverage of real UTME questions. We draw from past JAMB papers and educator-reviewed questions. The goal is 100% similarity in challenge level.

What subjects are covered? ▼

English Language is compulsory for all candidates. You choose 3 additional subjects from: Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Economics, Government, Literature, and Geography — matching the real JAMB subject list.

Is my data safe? ▼

Yes. Your account and exam data are stored securely on Google Firebase. Your payment is handled entirely by your bank — we never see your card or bank details. We do not sell or share your data with anyone.
